Everything You Need for Flawless Demos

Millisecond Keystroke Speed

Switch instantly between presets (Very Slow, Slow, Normal, Fast, Very Fast) or choose an exact delay down to 1 millisecond. Includes custom line-break and start delays.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Zero external servers, zero trackers, zero data collection. All text processing occurs in your local browser or editor memory. Suitable for proprietary code.

Indentation & Format Integrity

Tabs, nested indentations, multi-line blocks, empty lines, and special unicode symbols are reproduced with 100% pixel-perfect fidelity without corruption.

Google Chrome Extension

Manifest V3 • Works on every webpage
